Which Yoga Practice Is Right for You?

Classical Hatha Yoga programs in the lineage of Sadhguru — simple enough to begin tomorrow, profound enough to last a lifetime. Here is how to choose.

Upa Yoga

Simple 5-minute processes — "pre-yoga" for joints, energy, and stress relief

Surya Kriya

The original sun practice — health and spiritual depth in one

Surya Shakti

The dynamic, physical sun practice — power and stamina

Angamardana

Peak fitness using only your body — 31 processes

Yogasanas

84 classical postures — subtle processes, not exercise

Bhuta Shuddhi

Purification of the five elements — the practice beneath all practices

The 30-Second Chooser

  • Never done yoga? → Upa Yoga — "you cannot do it improperly — it is very simple!" — learnable in five minutes, free tools available
  • Want fitness from a classical source? → Angamardana or Surya Shakti — full fitness without equipment
  • Want one daily practice with both health and spiritual depth? → Surya Kriya — the original sun practice, "a complete spiritual process by itself"
  • Want the classical postures science? → Yogasanas — 84 postures, cosmic geometry, asana siddhi
  • Want the foundation beneath everything? → Bhuta Shuddhi — "every aspect of yoga that you do is just a small extraction from bhuta shuddhi practices"
  • Curious and committed? → Upa Yoga to start, then the deeper practices as interest deepens

What they all share

  • Learned properly, from a teacher — "Yoga is too subtle and refined a system to be imparted in words alone." Deeper practices are transmitted — "This cannot be taught in a book, because it is a process of transmission, not instruction." — and are then yours for life
  • Daily regularity multiplies everything — Sadhguru on practice: "If you are regular in your practice, you will see the way you experience life will be hundred-fold better. If you are irregular, you are back to square one."
  • No lifestyle change required — the practices were designed for ordinary people to gain heightened states of energy and perception without disrupting family and social life

Frequently asked

Do I need to be flexible? No — no flexibility or fitness level is required to learn any of these practices. They are taught step by step.

I'm too busy. Which one is smallest? Upa Yoga — individual processes take about five minutes and can be done anywhere, with no equipment.

I want fitness, but from a real tradition. Angamardana (whole-body mastery) or Surya Shakti (dynamic sun practice) — both use only the body, no equipment.

Can these practices be learned from videos or books? No — the deeper practices are processes of transmission: "This cannot be taught in a book, because it is a process of transmission, not instruction." They are learned directly from a trained teacher and then belong to you for life.
